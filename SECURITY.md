# Security

Report a vulnerability through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/charleslewisdev/loadout/security/advisories/new), not a public issue. That includes a secret or private term in the repo or its history, or a way past `guard/scan` or `guard/gh-hook`.

loadout runs as you, inside Claude Code: its hooks and scripts have your permissions. Read them before you install a fork you don't own.
