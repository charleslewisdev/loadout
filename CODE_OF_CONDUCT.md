# Code of conduct

loadout follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and don't harass anyone, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives.

**Reporting.** If someone's behavior worries you, tell me privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/charleslewisdev/loadout/security/advisories/new), and say it's a conduct report. Reports stay confidential. I may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who don't follow this code.
